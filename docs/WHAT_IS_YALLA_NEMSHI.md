# What is Yalla Nemshi?

**Yalla Nemshi** means "Let's walk!" in Arabic, and that's the whole idea: an app that
makes it easy to find people to walk with, or to start a walk and let others join you.

## The problem it solves

Walking is the easiest way to stay active, but it's more fun and easier to keep up with
company. Most people don't have a walking group, and group chats or social media
aren't made for organising a simple walk.

## How it works

- **Find a walk near you.** Open the app and see walks coming up nearby, as a list or on
  a map. Each walk shows when and where it starts, how long it is, the pace, and who's
  going. Tap **I'm in** to join.
- **Start your own walk.** Pick a place and a time and choose a route: from A to B, or a
  loop back to the start. It can be a one-off or repeat every week. Anyone nearby can
  join, or you can keep it **private** and share an invite code with friends only.
- **Walk together.** Each walk has a group chat for "running 5 minutes late" or "meet
  at the gate". When the walk starts, everyone taps **I'm here**, and the app records
  the route.
- **After the walk,** people rate the walk and the host, so good hosts stand out.

## What keeps people coming back

- **Your progress:** walks joined and hosted, kilometres walked, a weekly goal, a
  walking streak and badges.
- **Friends:** add people you've walked with and see what they're up to next.
- **Notifications** when someone joins your walk, a walk you joined changes, or you get
  a message.

## Safety and privacy

Your email is never shown to other users. Private walks stay hidden from strangers.
You can block or report anyone; a blocked person can't message you or send you friend
requests.

## Where it is today

The app is built for Android and iPhone and also runs in a web browser. It's in
testing before its first public release, and a fresh new design is being rolled out
screen by screen.
